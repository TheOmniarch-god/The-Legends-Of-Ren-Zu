Fast answers

The core questions people usually ask first

What is The Legends of Ren Zu?

It is a mythic narrative associated with Reverend Insanity that follows Ren Zu, the ancestor of humanity, through hardship, bargaining, revelation, and defiance.

Who is Ren Zu?

Ren Zu is the first human and the emotional center of the legends. He is the figure through whom the work explores loneliness, effort, loss, selfhood, and freedom.

What is Hope Gu?

Hope Gu is one of the most memorable symbolic Gu in the work. It repeatedly represents the ability to keep moving when strength, certainty, and comfort have already failed.

Do I need to read the chapters in order?

Yes. The cleanest path is from Part 1 onward, because later entries build on earlier symbols, family arcs, and conceptual threads.

Why does this site have separate chapter pages and an immersive reader?

The immersive reader is better for human experience. The static pages exist so search engines can crawl the material more clearly and readers can share stable URLs.

Why does Search Console say some URLs are unknown to Google?

That is normal right after sitemap submission and indexing requests. Google often needs time to crawl, process, and refresh the reporting layer.
